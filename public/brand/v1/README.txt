LOGOS
logo-horizontal-*: full horizontal lockup.
logo-vertical-*: stacked vertical lockup.
mark-*: standalone h with bookmark and book-spine cutout.
Use SVG for websites/print; PNG for platforms requiring raster artwork.
Ink/inverse retain the vermilion ribbon. Black/white are monochrome.
Inverse and white PNG files are transparent: preview them on a dark background.

ICONS
profile-1024.png / profile-512.png: branded social profile picture.
app-icon-192.png / app-icon-512.png: square app/platform icon.
app-icon-maskable-*: extra-safe padding for circular and platform masks.
apple-touch-icon.png: 180px iOS home-screen icon.
favicon.svg: optical small mark. favicon.ico: 16/32/48px entries.
favicon-16.png / favicon-32.png / favicon-48.png: raster browser icons.
social-card.png: branded 1200x630 share card.
identity-sheet.png: overview, not a logo to crop or embed as a header.

PALETTE
Ink #242321 / Paper #FAF7F0 / Vermilion #C75C3A.
Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, or recolour by section.
Keep clear space equal to one ribbon width (at least 1/8 mark height).
Minimum digital width: horizontal logo 136px, vertical logo 120px.
For smaller spaces use the optical favicon/app mark, not the full wordmark.
The favicon intentionally omits the fine spine slit for legibility.

SOURCE
Self-contained vector paths; no external font or image references.
Custom h geometry with Bodoni Moda-derived outlined lettering.
Font copyright/licence is in OFL.txt; source provenance and SHA256 hashes
are in identity.json. Site canonical address: https://theharithsa.com.
